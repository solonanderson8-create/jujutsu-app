Privacy

Jujutsu Flow · last updated 27 November 2026

In short: you don't need an account. Without one, everything stays on your device. If you sign in, a copy of your notes is kept in your account so it's the same on all your devices, and only you can see it.

Without an account

Your notes, journal, video links, flashcard progress and belt are saved in this browser on this device, and nowhere else. Clearing your browser's data deletes them.

What's stored if you sign in

  • Your email address (to send you sign-in codes).
  • Your move notes, training journal, video links, flashcard progress and belt setting, with the times you changed them.

Not stored: passwords (there are none, you sign in with an emailed code), payment details, your location, or your contacts. There are no ads and no tracking or analytics.

Your journal can be personal (injuries, how training felt, people you trained with). Write what you're comfortable keeping online.

Where it's kept

In a database run by Supabase, a cloud database company, on their servers. Connections are encrypted (HTTPS).

Who can see it

  • You, on any device where you sign in.
  • No other users. The database only ever hands a signed-in person their own records. This is enforced by the database itself and tested.
  • Honestly: the person who runs this app has administrator access to the database, which is needed to fix problems, so they could technically see stored data. They will not read your data except to fix a problem you report or if you ask them to. Supabase staff can access data only as described in their own privacy policy.

Other services the app uses

  • Google Fonts supplies the app's font, so Google sees that your device loaded it.
  • YouTube videos you add are shown with YouTube's privacy-enhanced player (youtube-nocookie.com). YouTube's own policy applies when you play one.

Your controls

  • Download a copy of all your data at any time (Account → "Download a copy of my data"). This works without an account too.
  • Sign out, and optionally remove your notes from that device (useful on a shared device).
  • Delete your account (Account → "Delete my account"). This immediately deletes your account and everything synced to it from the database. Copies may remain in the database provider's backups for a short time until those expire.

Age

If you're under 13, ask a parent or guardian before making an account.

Questions or changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes too. Questions: open an issue on the app's project page.
